# Charla keyboard (test version)

An Android keyboard for practising a language on your real messages. Type in your own
language, tap the language key, and build the translation by picking the right word from
the boxes above the keys. Then press send in your chat app as usual.

English to Spanish to start with; 59 languages can be chosen in the app.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open this link and download the file:
   **https://github.com/slinkybat123-code/charla-keyboard/releases/latest/download/charla.apk**
2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow your browser to install apps, then
   tap **Install**. If Play Protect offers to scan the app, let it, then install.
3. Open **Charla** and follow its two set-up steps (turn the keyboard on, then switch to it).
4. The first time, the phone downloads the language (about 30 MB each). It needs internet
   for that once.

Needs Android 8 or newer.

## Good to know

- This is a test version. A later version may ask you to uninstall this one first, which
  resets your level and progress.
- To go back to your usual keyboard, tap the globe key on Charla. To come back to Charla,
  use your phone's keyboard-switch button.
- Translation happens on the phone, with Google's ML Kit. Charla has no account and does not
  send what you type anywhere. Google's translation library downloads the language packs
  and may send Google anonymous usage statistics.
